SonoVive™ Ingredients

SonoVive™ is marketed as a dietary supplement designed to support hearing wellness and cognitive function. Online product pages list several herbal extracts and nutrients, but the reported formulas are not consistent across all websites. The ingredients below are reported on one available supplement label and should be checked against the bottle you intend to describe.

1

Ginkgo Biloba

Ginkgo biloba is a plant extract commonly used in dietary supplements for cognitive wellness and circulation. Researchers have studied it for several health applications, but evidence does not establish that SonoVive improves hearing loss or treats tinnitus.
2

St. John’s Wort

St. John’s wort is a flowering herb traditionally used in herbal preparations, particularly those related to mood. It may interact with many prescription medicines, including some antidepressants and hormonal contraceptives, so professional advice is important before use.
3

Bacopa Monnieri

Bacopa monnieri is a traditional herbal ingredient often included in supplements formulated for memory and cognitive wellness. Research has explored its potential effects on certain aspects of cognitive performance, but its benefits for hearing health have not been established.
4

L-Glutamine

L-glutamine is an amino acid involved in protein metabolism and several normal physiological processes. It is also associated with nitrogen transport in the body. Its inclusion in a supplement does not establish that it improves auditory function.
5

Phosphatidylserine

Phosphatidylserine is a phospholipid found naturally in cell membranes, including those of brain cells. It is commonly included in cognitive-support supplements. The evidence for specific benefits varies, and its effect on hearing remains uncertain.
6

N-Acetyl L-Carnitine

N-acetyl L-carnitine is a compound related to carnitine, which plays a role in cellular energy metabolism. It has been studied in different health contexts, but further evidence is needed to establish any meaningful benefit for hearing when used in SonoVive.
7

Vinpocetine

Vinpocetine is a compound associated with research into circulation and neurological function. Its safety deserves particular attention: it may not be appropriate for everyone, and the FDA has warned about potential risks during pregnancy. Consult a healthcare professional before taking products containing it.
8

Huperzine-A

Huperzine-A is a compound obtained from certain club mosses and is studied for its effects on acetylcholine, a chemical messenger involved in memory and other nervous-system functions. It can interact with medications and may cause adverse effects, so it should not automatically be considered safe simply because it is plant-derived.
